Obituary for COLLEEN GAY LATTIN
Publication Date: 20 Nov 2020 (Calgary, AB, CAN)
Obituary Content:
LATTIN, Colleen Gay December 22, 1961 - Calgary, Alberta November 5, 2020 - Calgary, Alberta

It is with great sorrow we announce Colleen Lattin, beloved daughter of David and Nancy Marchant of Calgary, AB, has lost her courageous battle with diabetes. She passed away on Thursday, November 5, 2020 at the age of 58 years.

Colleen was born in Calgary, AB on December 22, 1961 at the Calgary General Hospital, spending the majority of her life living in Calgary. She worked at Telus in city planning for fiber optic communications and was a member of the TWU-USW. Colleen accomplished much through her life and was particularly proud of the many years she dedicated to the merger of AGT and Telus. In 1988 she volunteered at the Olympics spending two weeks with her Father Dave Marchant, in which at one point she was awarded volunteer of the day with her photo in the newspaper. Colleen enjoyed vacationing at Mazatlan where she frequented often and rescued one of her two dogs from there.

Colleen is survived by her parents, David and Nancy, her brother Brett (Christine); her nephews, Shawn and Aaron; and her extended family, aunt Diane; the Bohn Family, Steve, Terry, Shannon, aunt Ann of Seattle; the Ware family, Cathy, Alan and Sheryl of Seattle; the Moore family, Jim and Bill of Whitehorse; Cindy, Don-Ann, and aunt Donna of Calgary. She was predeceased by her uncles, Lloyd, Roger and Jim; aunts, Bev and Carol and her cousin Debbie.

Funeral Services will be held at McINNIS & HOLLOWAY (Fish Creek, 14441 Bannister Road SE, Calgary, AB) on Saturday, November 21, 2020 at 2:00 p.m. Condolences, memories and photos may be shared and viewed on Colleen's obituary at www.McInnisandHolloway.com.

In living memory of Colleen Lattin, a tree will be planted in the Ann & Sandy Cross Conservation Area.
